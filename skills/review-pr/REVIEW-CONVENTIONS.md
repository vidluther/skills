# Review conventions

My preferences for reviewing other people's PRs. `review-pr` reads this every run. Grow it as habits form — one line per rule.

## Comment voice

- Direct and specific; say what to change and why in one or two sentences.
- Ask, don't assert, when I might be missing context ("Is X intentional? It looks like Y happens when…").
